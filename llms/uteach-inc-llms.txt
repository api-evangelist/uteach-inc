# Uteach, Inc.

> Uteach (uteach.io) is an all-in-one online teaching platform and LMS for creating a branded website and selling online courses, coaching sessions, live lessons, quizzes with certificates, digital products, and community memberships — also positioned as a secure LMS for employee training. It publishes no public developer portal or OpenAPI; its programmatic surface is an integration API key (plus school domain) for Zapier/Make/Pabbly automation, an SSO user-provisioning endpoint with a bearer token, and 18 instant event triggers delivered through those automation platforms.
